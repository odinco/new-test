The recipe for fun but professional

Every page in this lookbook follows the same few rules. Steal them for your own reports, invoices, and flyers.

Cover · Sunny pop
Report · Garden
Invoice · Grape
Event · Night
Proposal · Primary
01
Play with one thing

A big headline, one bright color, or one shape. Keep everything else calm and on a grid.

02
Warm, off-white paper

Cream, lilac, or ink-navy backgrounds feel designed. Plain white can feel like a default.

03
Round the corners

Soft 14–22px radii on cards and chips feel friendly without being childish.

04
Use a mono label voice

Small uppercase monospace for labels and metadata adds precision next to playful display type.

05
Let numbers be heroes

Show key figures large, with tabular digits and a single accent. Readers trust what they can scan.

06
Write like a human

"Thanks for being lovely to work with!" does more for personality than any illustration.
