Things to try

  • Stop with coil A on the 100% hole. The differential has drawn one loop. Keep pulling and coil B draws the second loop on the other side of null.
  • Stretch the wear scar past the coil spacing. The differential splits into two small signals at the scar's ends while the absolute stays one hump.
  • Widen the coil spacing. Short flaws get a fuller differential response; long flaws still split.
  • Tick "Mirrored lobes". Same 40° transition, loops on the wrong sides of the line.
